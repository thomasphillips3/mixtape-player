## 🎨 **Branding Best Practices**

### **Required Assets**
- **App Icon**: 1024x1024px PNG, artist logo or album art
- **Album Artwork**: High-resolution (at least 1000x1000px)
- **Background Image**: Optional custom background
- **Color Scheme**: Primary, secondary, accent colors

### **Professional Presentation**
- Include your studio branding alongside artist branding
- Add technical specs to track metadata
- Include mixing notes and credits
- Professional documentation template

## 🔧 **Technical FAQs for Clients**

### **Installation Process**
- **Android**: Enable "Unknown Sources" → Install APK → Launch
- **iOS**: TestFlight invitation → Install → Enjoy

### **Audio Quality**
- **Format**: FLAC 96kHz/24-bit (or client's preference)
- **Quality**: Bit-perfect reproduction of your masters
- **Compatibility**: All modern devices and vehicles

### **Vehicle Integration**
- **Android Auto**: Automatic discovery, full browsing
- **CarPlay**: Native iOS integration, Siri support
- **Requirements**: Compatible vehicle or aftermarket head unit

## 💡 **Advanced Features**

### **Premium Add-Ons**
- **Custom UI themes** matching artist branding
- **Enhanced metadata** with detailed mixing notes
- **Multi-format support** (FLAC + MP3 versions)
- **App Store distribution** for wider reach

### **Future Enhancements**
- **Analytics dashboard** showing listening habits
- **Social features** for fan engagement
- **Streaming integration** for wider catalog
- **Live performance integration**

## 📞 **Support & Troubleshooting**

### **Common Issues**
- **App won't install**: Check device settings, enable unknown sources
- **Android Auto not appearing**: Verify USB connection, restart Android Auto
- **Audio quality concerns**: Confirm source file quality, check device capabilities

### **Client Support Template**
Provide clients with:
- **Installation guide** with screenshots
- **Troubleshooting checklist**
- **Your contact information** for technical support
- **Feature overview** explaining capabilities
